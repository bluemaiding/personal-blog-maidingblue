When...

2025 · 现代诗 · 现代文 · English · 387+741 字 · 约 5 分钟

When…

maiding blue

Where have you gone?

To the old street corner beyond the sunset's glow,

Or the seashore, lined with coco?

When the grapes on the vines are already ripe,

When the last bird wings south without looking back,

When winter's first snowflake starts its gentle flight,

Covering streets and alleys, as promised ahead,

As the setting sun sheds its final glow,

Gazing at the lone pedestrian below.

I embark on my journey,

though I know it is a boundless quest,

Year after year, never will I rest.

When the early spring breeze blows across the plain,

I'll gather a basket of glittering cherry petals,

To bring you the first glimpse of

The south's earliest springtime tales.

When midsummer's rain wets your short hair,

I'll mount a purely-white-painted bicycle,

Racing down the golden rape-flowered field.

When late autumn's longing fills the noisy campus,

I will pick the passionately reddest wutong leaf,

Let it slowly grow yellow in my memory's ashes.

When winter's snowflakes fall in a flurry,

Just like our youth, so free and merry.

I should look up at the snow, they leaning down to say:

"Another winter has come, still one less today?"

After a long, long time, I come to know,

What I have been chasing

Is never you,

Never;

But always myself,

Always.

When the last New Zealand aurora fades away,

Ended its enchanting encounter with the earth, or rather date;

The dream of traveling the world seems so empty,

A blank canvas, a naive memory.

When the last spark’s sparkle comes to a halt,

When all is silent, like the deep-sea reaching out:

The distant lighthouse cast beams of wavering light,

With nearby waves ebbing and flowing, in and out of the sight.

Will you still remember those sparkling days?

That passed, like fleeting haze.

The birds of that day will probably return,

Maybe tomorrow, or after a long turn?

But the color of that forever dusk,

will never be the same again.

Don't linger in the past's bubble-like dreams!

They break so easily, like passing streams.

There are biting and wicked wind in the road ahead,

Only the moon knows how grand and eventful

the journey will end.

The once-expected ever-lasting joyous song,

The once-unforgettable passion, all along,

Now, looking back, are just like winter's snow,

——Nothing special, after all.

当……

你到底去了哪里，

是余晖不及的老街角落，

还是长满椰树的海岸边？

当满藤的葡萄都已熟透；

当最后的一只飞鸟都已，

头也不回地直飞向南方；

当今年冬天的第一片雪，

如约而至落在大街小巷；

夕阳洒下最后一抹余晖，

静静凝视街上孤单行人。

我开始寻找了，

虽然漫无边际，

纵使年复一年。

当早春的和风吹过广阔的平原，

我要捡一篮

沾满露滴的樱花瓣，

为你捎去南国最早的春光明媚；

当盛夏的大雨打湿你额前短发，

我要骑一辆

刷着白色油漆的自行车，

在那开满油菜花的金黄山野驰骋；

当深秋的想念一片一片

落满人来人往的喧闹校园，

我要挑一片

红得最热烈的梧桐叶

让它在我记忆深处的灰烬里

慢慢泛黄；

当冬日的雪花又一次

纷纷扬扬地落下，

如此潇洒，像我们的青春一样；

我抬头看雪，雪俯身问我：

“又是一年冬来到，

遍坐桌前少一人？”

好久好久以后，

我才慢慢领悟，

我一直追寻的，

一直，不是你，

一直，都是我。

当最后的极光在新西兰

结束了它与地球的邂逅；

环游地球的幻想

才发觉它空白得可怜；

当最后一缕火焰也不再跳动，

当一切都像无边深海

归于沉寂：

远处的灯塔扑朔迷离，

近处的波涛若即若离。

你还会记得吗？

那些迷人的日子。

那天的飞鸟总会回来吧，

或许是明天，或许要更久？

但那个黄昏的颜色，

却再也不会一样了。

别再沉湎于过去的泡沫，

别忘它们一触就破。

前方的路山风凌冽，

只有明月才会知道

这趟旅途将多壮烈。

曾经期待的椒花颂声，

曾经难忘的轰轰烈烈，

如今，再回首看去，

不过就像冬天的雪，

——终究没什么特别。

后记：单曲循环了一晚上邓紫棋的《泡沫》、Avril的《Innocence》，还有Taylor的《Enchanted》，借着莫名的灵感，几乎是一气呵成地完成初稿。人生海海，山山而川，或许真的只有不破不立，才会晓喻新生。值得注意的是，做出选择从来没有错；但是，可能真的唯有时间才能给出答案。

Speak out loud now, don’t you ever wait.

就算迎接昔日炽热的轰轰烈烈的终将是形同陌路的欲言又止，但至少我们曾来过。

与这郁郁葱葱而轰轰烈烈的岁月共勉。
